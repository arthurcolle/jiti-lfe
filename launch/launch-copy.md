## What should make the demonstration interesting

1. **Visible accumulation.** The catalogue begins without expense functions and grows while the process remains running. Each definition is inspectable and immediately usable.
2. **Concrete composition.** The report calls functions created earlier. Show those calls and the exact money totals so the viewer can follow the data.
3. **Evidence of live continuation.** Keep the operation identity, original frame marker, and entry count visible across the pause and resume. Then show the next call entering the replacement body.
4. **A durable artifact.** Restart the process and call recovered functions against the recovered ledger. Explain precisely what persisted.
5. **An experiment readers can challenge.** Provide the caller checks, rollback behavior, adapter limitations, and raw capture. Link the one-command offline reproduction prominently.

The opening should deliver the first useful result within ten seconds when capture pacing permits. Remove model waiting time transparently with an “accelerated” or “waiting shortened” marker and retain the uncut source. Avoid a long animated logo introduction.
